## Brand & Style

This design system targets Korean engineering students preparing for the National Technical Qualification Examination in Electrical Engineering (전기기사 - 전력공학). The experience is strictly optimized for handheld, single-thumb portrait interactions during commutes, meals, and quick review intervals.

The brand style balances utilitarian clarity with the precision of technical drafts:
- **Distraction-Free Focus:** High signal-to-noise ratio. Complex technical schematics, mathematical formulas, and Korean Hangul typography take precedence over decorative flair.
- **Warm Technical Paper Aesthetic:** Off-white, soft paper-slate backdrops eliminate blue-light glare and eye strain during prolonged night-time cram sessions.
- **High-Voltage Accents:** Crisp cobalt blues paired with energetic amber sparks convey electrical authority, technical rigour, and high mental readiness.
- **Ergonomic Thumb-Zone Architecture:** Primary action controls (submit, skip, mark wrong, advance) are anchored strictly in the lower 35% of the viewport to facilitate effortless one-handed thumb navigation.

## Colors

The color architecture is built for rapid feedback and cognitive clarity:

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`):** Deep electric cobalt blue. Conveys engineering rigor, precision, and focus. Used for primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, key formulas, and primary question numbers.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`):** Warm electric amber. Signals high priority, tricky review questions, formula book alerts, and ambiguous/re-check statuses.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#059669`):** Emerald green. Explicitly reserved for correct answers, verified mastery badges, and positive progression metrics.
- **Status / Error (`#E11D48`):** Vivid rose red. Highlights incorrect submissions, mistake counter badges, and critical warning notes.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:** 
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50 / warm drafting paper).
  - Elevated surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white card surfaces).
  - Subtle borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200).
- **Text & Contrast:** 
  - Primary text: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) ensuring AAA contrast for complex Korean characters and subscript formulas.
  - Secondary text: `#475569` (Slate 600) for metadata, exam years, and chapter classifications.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes Korean legibility, mathematical equations, and quick scanning while moving:

- **Typefaces:** Plus Jakarta Sans is paired system-wide for both Latin and Hangul glyph fallbacks (serving Pretendard/system sans-serif), offering open counters, tall x-heights, and distinct character terminals. JetBrains Mono is utilized specifically for technical annotations, question indexing (e.g., `Q.08 [2023-1회]`), formula constants, and numerical score counters.
- **Korean Reading Dynamics:** Korean technical terms require balanced leading. Body copy uses generous 1.6–1.65 line heights (`28px` on `17px` body) to prevent dense mathematical notations and Hanja-derived electrical terms from clustering visually.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - `headline-lg` & `headline-md`: Dedicated to chapter titles, score summaries, and modal sheets.
  - `body-lg`: The primary reading scale for examination questions and formula derivations to preserve legibility at arm's length.
  - `body-md`: Detailed answer rationales, calculation breakdowns, and technical explanations.
  - `label-md` & `label-sm`: Micro-badges, examination session tags, and timer readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built strictly for mobile portrait displays with a maximum viewport constraint of 440px:

- **Grid & Margins:** A single-column vertical layout bounded by a constant `1rem` (16px) outer canvas margin. Content never bleeds to the edge except for full-bleed answer review sheets.
- **Rhythm & Touch Geometry:** 
  - Standard spacing rhythm follows a 4px/8px modular base. 
  - Gap between multiple-choice items is pinned to `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px), preventing mis-taps.
  - Primary button containers require a fixed minimum height between 52px and 56px to guarantee effortless thumb taps while walking.
- **Bottom-Bar Clearance:** The bottom sticky zone is reserved for sticky actions and bottom tabs, with a mandatory safe padding of `calc(1rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` to prevent interference with mobile home gestures.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, clean tonal surfaces combined with delicate ambient shadows, mirroring structured paper flashcards:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Warm paper base `#F8FAFC`.
  - **Level 1 (Card/Container):** Pure `#FFFFFF` with a subtle outline of `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - **Level 2 (Active/Selected Card):** Pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 2px active ring in cobalt `#1D4ED8` or error rose `#E11D48`.
  - **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar & Modals):** Pure `#FFFFFF` with an upward diffused ambient shadow (`0 -4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- **Shadow Philosophy:** Hard, skeuomorphic drop-shadows are strictly avoided. Depth is conveyed through subtle borders and ultra-diffused, slate-tinted ambient occlusion (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`), ensuring cards feel lightweight and responsive to quick thumb flicks.

## Shapes

The interface employs a unified `rounded-2xl` aesthetic for cards and tactile elements:

- **Cards & Sheets:** Base radius is set to 1rem (16px / `rounded-2xl`), delivering friendly, soft physical card metaphors that clearly demarcate individual exam questions and answer reviews.
- **Primary Buttons:** Unified at 0.75rem to 1rem (12px to 16px) corner radius to match card containers while maintaining solid, thumb-friendly hit targets.
- **Status Badges & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for categorical distinction between structural cards and state tags.

## Components

### 1. Multiple-Choice Option Items
- **Structure:** Full-width selectable card rows with minimum height of 56px. Left-aligned option index circle (`①`, `②`, `③`, `④`), centered Korean option copy, right-aligned feedback indicator.
- **Default State:** White background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, Slate 900 text.
- **Selected State:** Light cobalt tint `#EFF6FF`, 2px border `#1D4ED8`, cobalt text.
- **Correct Feedback State:** Light emerald tint `#ECFDF5`, 2px border `#059669`, emerald icon.
- **Incorrect Feedback State:** Light rose tint `#FFF1F2`, 2px border `#E11D48`, rose icon.

### 2. Action Buttons (Thumb Anchored)
- **Primary Bottom CTA:** 56px height, width 100%, background `#1D4ED8`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold `17px`. Active state yields a subtle 0.98 scale transform for tactile confirmation.
- **Secondary / Action Splits:** Dual-action layout in bottom thumb zone for "모름 (Skip/Unknown)" in neutral slate border (`#E2E8F0`) and "정답 확인 (Submit)" in solid cobalt.

### 3. Question & Review Cards
- **Container:** Pure white, 16px border-radius (`rounded-2xl`), 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 16px padding.
- **Header Slot:** JetBrains Mono pill badge for session tag (e.g., `2023년 2회 기출`), problem number in cobalt headline, and bookmark/star toggle icon on top-right.
- **Body Slot:** Question statement with explicit `lineHeight: 28px`.
- **Explanation Accordion:** Collapsible drawer with light warm-slate background (`#F1F5F9`) detailing calculation step-by-step formulas in JetBrains Mono.

### 4. Status Badges & Chips
- **Correct Badge:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` label, 4px vertical padding, 10px horizontal padding, pill-shaped.
- **Incorrect Badge:** `#FFF1F2` background, `#E11D48` label, pill-shaped.
- **Re-Study (오답보류) Badge:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` label, pill-shaped.

### 5. Sticky Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Architecture:** Fixed to bottom viewport, 64px height + safe-area inset. 2 primary tabs:
  1. `문제 풀기` (Solving / Quiz icon)
  2. `오답노트` (Incorrect Review / Notebook icon with red badge counter)
- **Visuals:** Pure white surface, top border 1px `#E2E8F0`, active tab in `#1D4ED8` with a 3px active dot indicator, inactive tab in `#64748B`.

### 6. Formula & Schematic Preview Box
- Inset container inside cards with 8px radius, border `1px dashed #CBD5E1`, background `#F8FAFC`, presenting technical diagrams and LaTeX-formatted electrical formulas with horizontal scroll support.
